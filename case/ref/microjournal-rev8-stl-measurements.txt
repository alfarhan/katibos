Notes that mattered to the katibOS design
-----------------------------------------
* Four parts are exactly 200.0 in X. That is a deliberate bed cap, not a
  coincidence -- Ender/Prusa-Mini class. Adopted for Deck Mk1.
* The base is 47.1 tall because it houses six subassemblies (ESP32-S3, LiPo
  charger/step-up, 18650 holder, rocker switch, keyboard PCB, FPC adapter).
  One Waveshare board replaces all of them, which is the whole reason Deck
  Mk1's base can be 28.
* The lid is only 12 thick because it carries glass alone; the display ribbon
  therefore crosses the hinge. Deck Mk1 inverts this -- the lid is the entire
  computer and nothing crosses the hinge.
* Hood Left/Right (95.2 x 106.7) + Hood Top (144 x 11) form a three-piece
  shroud around the glass. rev.8 hoods a reflective panel and its author's
  pitch is that the panel gets better in bright light: both hold, because a
  raised frame on a lid leaning ~78 deg away from the reader shades nothing.
  The rule is "no hood on a steep panel", not "no hood".
* BOM (build.md) calls for M3 x 70 screws -- those are the hinge pins.
  Also M3/M2 heat-set inserts, B-7000 glue, and a TORX T10H driver.
